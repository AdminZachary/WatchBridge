# WatchBridge 0.1.8

Apple Watch installation is reworked: transfers adapt to the observed write
pressure, and the install now shows its progress. The design notes and caveats are
in #17.

- A 200 MB IPA installs in about two minutes over Wi-Fi
- Six stages, each with a percentage, plus a live transfer rate
- Per-step timeouts, a three-minute no-progress watchdog, and retries only for
  transient errors

Thanks to @seomin0610, whose analysis of the iPhone-side buffering in #8 is what
pointed us at the problem.

## Requirements

**Windows** — Windows 10 22H2 or Windows 11 (x64), Apple Mobile Device Service and the
WebView2 runtime.

**macOS** — macOS 11 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel, without Xcode.

**Linux** — `usbmuxd`, WebKitGTK 4.1, a Secret Service keyring and xdg-desktop-portal.

An iPhone paired with the target Watch is required; preparation needs a cable.

## Known limitations

- The transfer controller is a sender heuristic, not a measurement of Watch
  acknowledgement; the Wi-Fi path cannot be confirmed from the API.
- The Windows build is unsigned, so SmartScreen warns on first run. The macOS bundle
  is ad-hoc signed and not notarized: allow it once under **System Settings → Privacy
  & Security → Open Anyway**, then open the app again.
- One paired Watch per iPhone; only IPAs containing both the iPhone host and
  `Watch/*.app` are supported.
